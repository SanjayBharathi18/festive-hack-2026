Icons — round 2: line with one fill

One idea: the fill shows what the object is made of. Most of these objects are steel: the tiffin, the thali, the handi. On the kaju katli, the fill is the silver vark.

What changed from round 1: you picked line with one fill, and it looked like Claude. Anthropic's illustrations are black line art with flat, muted beige fills on cream, and the brass tint on cotton paper was exactly that. Option a in each control is the round 1 pick. Each other option changes one thing: the fill colour, the drawing, or the ground.

How to choose: on your phone, try b · Steel and b · Redraw first, then look at section 05 at arm's length. Pick the one you like that reads fastest.

Selected: a · Brass tint with c · Your pick. That is the redraw for every icon, except Catering and Bulk, which keep draft 1. The page opens on the selection.

06NotesWhat each option does, and what happens next.

  • a · Brass tint: the round 1 fill. Beige on cream, which is why it read as Claude.
  • b · Steel: a cool grey from the steel the objects are made of. It stands apart from the warm paper, so the page no longer reads as cream and beige.
  • c · Haldi: warm and festive. But haldi is the colour of the primary action, so filled icons start to look like buttons, and on a haldi button the fill disappears.
  • d · White: each icon becomes a paper cut-out. On the white ground it turns into a plain line icon.
  • White ground: the whole page on white instead of cotton paper. It tests whether the cream ground adds to the Claude look.
  • Next: you pick a fill and a drawing. I refine the set, check each icon at 24 px on a phone, and copy it to src/icons/ as SVG files.
