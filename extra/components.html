Components

One idea: this page is the app's own look, not a swatch. It loads only the production files: src/styles/main.css, the fonts in src/fonts and the icon sprite src/public/icons.svg. If a component looks wrong here, it looks wrong in the app.

What changed from your selection: nothing in the look. New, because no swatch had them: the non-veg mark, the status badges, the form fields, the busy mark, the pressed link button, and the disabled plain and link buttons. The plain and link buttons are now 56 px tall, like every tap target. Then, from the layout reference: the app is a laptop website, so controls are 44 px tall, buttons and chips are fully round, inputs have 8 px corners, and cards and order slips have 16 px corners (section 16 later made these corners squircles). Section 13 shows the new laptop pieces.

How to check: open it on your phone, at arm's length. Each block names a component. Each caption gives the state and its exact spec.

16Squircle corners

Every card of the site takes Apple's squircle, a superellipse with continuous curvature, and the buttons stay fully round, as on apple.com's product pages. One rule in base.css sets corner-shape: squircle on every element; pills and circles keep round ends. A squircle cuts less deep than an arc of the same radius, so each token is calibrated larger, by eye, against apple.com's 28 px and 18 px card arcs. Chromium draws the squircle; another browser draws the plain radius.

apple.com: a 28 px arc

28 px squircle: too square

48 px squircle (--radius-card): the same weight

Large: 48 px. Dialogs and other large surfaces. Reason: the arc and the squircle cut the corner to the same depth, and the squircle's curve starts earlier, so it reads organic, not drawn with a compass.

apple.com: an 18 px arc

Small: 32 px (--radius-card-small) for cards, slips, panels and the cart bar; 24 px (--radius-input) for inputs and the boxes inside a card. A 44 px field caps the corner at half its height, which reads as Apple's 12 px field.

Sweets Paid Recorded

Icon tile: 41% (--radius-tile, first): Apple's icon corner of 22.37% of the width, times 1.84 for the squircle. The second tile is a plain 22.37% squircle: too square. Buttons, chips, tabs and selects keep round ends. Stamps stay square; badges keep a round 4 px corner.
