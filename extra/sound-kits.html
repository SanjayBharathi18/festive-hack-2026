Sound kits — audition

The app makes a few short sounds: when you tap +, when an order is saved, when a new order reaches the desk. This page lets you hear three Indian kits for the same moments. Each kit keeps the grammar of Spaceship's sound (your hand makes short marks, the app answers in tones, a settled state ends clean, pitch shows a value) and replaces its sci-fi clicks with acoustic sounds tuned to one raag. Every sound is made here in code, with no audio files.

Start with the scenarios: each one plays the same 10-second story in one kit. Then play single sounds and select the one you like on each row. A row can take its sound from any kit. On a laptop, turn on Phone speaker: most people will hear these sounds through a small phone speaker.

    05grammar
    
        
    
          
    Pitch shows a value
    
          
    The pack sound climbs the raag as the quantity goes up, and falls as it goes down.
    
          
    One raag
    
          
    Every pitch comes from one pentatonic raag in just intonation, so sounds that overlap never clash.
    
          
    Silence while the mic is open
    
          
    The start mark plays before the mic opens, and the stop mark after it closes. Nothing plays in between.
    
          
    One big moment
    
          
    "Order saved" is the only long sound, about 670 ms: close to the one long motion on the screen.
    
          
    Background is silent
    
          
    The daily list, background saves and search results make no sound.
