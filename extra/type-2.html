Type — round 2: words with mono numbers

One idea: numbers read like a scale readout, in mono. The words must sit well next to them, in all six languages, at arm's length in a sunny kitchen.

What changed from round 1: you kept mono for numbers and liked Anek. In both pickers, option a is that pick: IBM Plex Mono with Anek. Each other option changes one thing, either the numbers or the words. Every mono here has the ₹ sign, checked in its font file.

How to choose: on your phone, change one picker at a time. Pick the numbers first in section 02, then the words in section 01, then check the pair in sections 03 and 05.

Selected: a · Anek, at normal width, for words, with a · IBM Plex Mono for numbers.
