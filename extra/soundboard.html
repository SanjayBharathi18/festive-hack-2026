Soundboard — one family

Every sound of the app, built from docs/sound.md. The tabla is your hand. Counter objects (the bill-book slip, the ink pad, the rubber stamp) mark a commitment. Steel (katori, tumbler, chai glass, tiffin latch, bicycle bell) is the app answering. Your "slip, stamp, katori" is the sound logo, and other sounds quote it. All pitches are Bhupali on E, all sounds play in one small room, and each sound has the loudness of its tier: the more often it plays, the quieter it is. Everything is synthesised here. There are no audio files.

Positive sounds share one ending: a katori on Pa, then a katori on Sa that rings. Negative sounds share one gesture: the bayan falls and stops. So "it worked" is high, steel and ringing, and "it did not" is low, skin and muted.

How to listen: on a laptop, turn on Phone speaker. Play A day at the counter first, then the positive and the negative sounds. Then use the controls under 02 try it, the way you would use the app. The takes marked "in the build" are the team's picks. To try another take, select it under 03 roles. Takes marked "new" are from the last round.
