Type — six versions

One idea: Indian scripts are first-class. The type must set an item name in two scripts at the same size, keep prices in straight columns, and stay clear at arm's length in a sunny kitchen.

Fixed from your answers: the palette, light, roomy, 4 px corners, plain slips, red for "to do" and green for "done". Only the typefaces change from A to F. F is today's default, the system font, for comparison.

How to choose: open this page on your phone, tap a version at the top, and read sections 02 to 04 at arm's length, in daylight. Pick the one you like that reads fastest. If two tie, take the faster one. The Kannada, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu and Marathi lines are samples. A native speaker checks them before the app uses them.
