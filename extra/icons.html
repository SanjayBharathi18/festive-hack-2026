Icons — three styles

One idea: an icon helps a word, and it must read at 24 px on a phone in a sunny kitchen. The set is drawn for this app from Indian working objects: a tiffin carrier for the cart, kaju katli for sweets, a samosa for snacks, a chakli spiral for savouries, a thali for meals, a handi for catering, a cloth bag for pickup and a scooter for delivery.

Fixed from your answers: the palette, 4 px corners and roomy controls. Only the drawing style changes. All three styles are one drawing: CSS sets the style from one data attribute.

How to choose: open this page on your phone, tap a style at the top, and look at section 04 at arm's length. Pick the one you like that reads fastest. These are first drafts: after you pick, I redraw the set in that style.

05NotesWhat each style does, and what happens next.

  • a · Solid, stamp-cut: filled ink shapes with the details cut out. The heaviest, so it reads best at 24 px in sun, and it echoes the rubber stamps. On this page the cut-outs are drawn in the colour behind the icon. The final set uses real cut-outs.
  • b · Fine line: 1.75 px ink lines with round ends. The lightest and most minimal. At a glance it can pass for a stock line set.
  • c · Line with one fill: the same lines, with one flat brass-tint fill on the main surface. The tint appears nowhere else in the app, so it does not take a job from another colour.
  • The FSSAI veg and non-veg marks are not part of this set. They follow the regulation exactly, in every style.
  • Next: you pick a style. I redraw all 20 icons in it, check each one at 24 px on a phone, and copy the set to src/icons/ as SVG files, as extra/README.md says.
