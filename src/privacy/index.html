Privacy

Plattr uses your data only for your order.

Your data
Your WhatsApp number, your name, the delivery address, your note, and the name and phone of a gift's receiver.
Why
Only for this order and its updates on WhatsApp. The cook sees it so that she can make and send your order.
How long
Your order stays with the orders of the store. At a demo store, every order is deleted each night, when the demo starts again. This browser keeps your name and number for your next order.
Stop
To withdraw your consent, reply STOP to a message from us, or message the store.
Complain
Write to the store on WhatsApp: use Message the store in a message from us or on your order page. If that does not solve it, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
